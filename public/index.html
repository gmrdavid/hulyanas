Our Story

Nestled in the beautiful hills of Southern Leyte, Hulyanas Hill brings authentic home-style cooking using fresh local ingredients. Our family recipes have been perfected over generations.

Every dish tells a story of our heritage, prepared with love and served with pride at two convenient locations in Silago and Hinunangan.
